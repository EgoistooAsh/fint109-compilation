The problem

Moving money across borders still costs too much

Every day, people send money home, get paid by clients overseas or spend abroad. Each time, part of it disappears along the way.

Example (fictional): send $500 the usual way and your family might receive about $473, one to two days later.

  • Transfer fees

    A charge just to send.

  • Exchange-rate markups

    A worse rate than the real one, hidden inside the conversion.

  • Waiting time

    Days before the money can be used.

How it works

How Kova works

Five steps from your card to anywhere in the world.

  1. Top up

    Add money with a debit card, credit card or bank transfer.

  2. Hold digital dollars

    Kova converts it to USDC, a stablecoin worth 1 US dollar, and shows your balance in your home currency.

  3. Spend anywhere

    Pay with the Kova virtual card or scan a local QR code. The merchant receives local currency.

  4. Send in minutes

    Transfer to any Kova user worldwide for a flat $0.50.

  5. Cash out

    Move money to a bank account or local e-wallet when you need it.

Features

Built for money that crosses borders

Earn, spend and send across currencies from one app.

One digital-dollar balance

Your money is held in USDC, so its value tracks the US dollar while you decide when to convert.

Pay like a local

Use the virtual card online or in stores, or scan local QR codes such as QR Ph, PromptPay, PayNow, UPI and Pix.

Send in minutes

Wallet-to-wallet transfers settle on a low-fee blockchain network. You see the flat fee before you confirm.

Cash out locally

Withdraw to a bank account or local e-wallet in the receiver's currency.

Kova Invest Optional

Buy and hold Bitcoin or Ether in a separate tab. Payments always come from your USDC balance, so crypto price swings never change what you pay at checkout.

Protected by design

Identity checks at sign-up, biometric login, two-factor authentication and a one-tap card freeze.
