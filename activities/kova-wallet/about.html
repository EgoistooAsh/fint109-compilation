The technology

Why stablecoins, not Bitcoin, for everyday payments

A stablecoin is a digital token built to hold a steady value, usually 1 US dollar, backed by reserves of cash and cash equivalents. Bitcoin and Ether are useful investments, but their prices move too much for paying a bill.

Trust

Safety and compliance

Kova is a concept, but a real launch would have to meet the same rules as any money service. Here is what that would take.

  • Licensed market by market. There is no single global license. In the Philippines, crypto services are licensed by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) (opens in a new tab) as virtual asset service providers (VASPs); in the EU, under the MiCA regulation.
  • Know Your Customer (KYC). Every user verifies their identity before sending or cashing out.
  • Anti-money laundering (AML) monitoring. Transfers are screened for suspicious patterns and sanctioned parties.
  • The Travel Rule. Sender and receiver details travel with each transfer, following standards set by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) (opens in a new tab).
  • Reserve checks. Kova only supports stablecoins whose reserves are independently checked every month.
  • Account security. Biometric login, two-factor authentication and a one-tap card freeze.

Note: The BSP has paused new VASP licenses for an indefinite period, so a real Philippine launch would have to work through an already-licensed provider.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Kova Wallet a real product?

No. It's a fictional concept made for a university class.

Do shops need to accept crypto?

No. Kova converts at checkout, so the merchant receives its local currency.

What if USDC drops below $1?

Stablecoins can briefly trade below their peg during a market shock. Kova would warn users and pause conversions until the price recovered.

Can I keep Bitcoin or Ether?

Yes, in the optional Kova Invest tab. Payments always come from your USDC balance.

Where does Kova work?

In any market where it holds a license. The five QR systems show the first regions it was designed for.
